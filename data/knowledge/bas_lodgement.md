# BAS lodgement frequency

Most small businesses lodge their Business Activity Statement (BAS) quarterly.
Businesses with GST turnover of $20 million or more must report GST monthly.
Some businesses choose monthly reporting to manage cash flow or refunds.
A change in turnover can change the reporting obligation, so the assigned
accountant should review the client's latest turnover before advising.
Always confirm the client's current obligations in ATO Online Services before
giving a final answer. This is general information, not tax advice.
